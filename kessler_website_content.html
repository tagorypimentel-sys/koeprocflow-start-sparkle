Services Page — Engineering

Engineering | Design | Fabrication | Mounting

"Some companies maintain equipment. Kessler Offshore engineers it."

From Drawing to Operation — Complete Engineering Solutions

Kessler Offshore delivers end-to-end engineering solutions covering the full project lifecycle from initial concept to onboard commissioning.

01 — Engineering & Design

Every project begins with rigorous technical development. Our engineering team produces detailed 3D CAD models and structural drawings — including FEA (Finite Element Analysis) — applying mechanical and naval engineering principles to ensure every component is optimized for load, space, safety, and long-term durability in marine environments.

02 — Steel Fabrication

From certified drawings to finished structure. We handle the full fabrication of metallic structures in-house — cutting, welding, forming, and finishing — with strict dimensional control and quality inspection at every stage. Materials include Aluminium, Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, and high-strength alloys such as Duplex and Superduplex. ASME and ASTM standards. Compliant with DNV, Lloyd's and ABS classification requirements.

03 — Assembly & Integration

A structure alone is not a system. Our technical team handles the complete assembly and integration of all mechanical and electrical components — motors, drive systems, electrical panels, control units, safety devices, and ancillary accessories — ensuring every element functions as a unified, reliable whole before the equipment reaches the vessel.

04 — Onboard Installation

We mobilize directly to the vessel, managing the full installation process in coordination with the ship's crew, port authorities, and classification inspectors. Rope access and rigging teams enable installation in difficult-to-access locations — without scaffolding.

05 — Commissioning & Handover

The project is only complete when the equipment performs. We conduct full functional testing under operational conditions and hand over with complete technical documentation — ready for immediate use.

📌 All class certifications and NDT certifications are presented through a detailed databook at the end of each scope.

Project Examples

  • Window Washing Machine — Full engineering design, fabrication, and commissioning for cruise line vessel (RCCL). CAD design → fabrication → onboard installation → operational.
  • Custom Access Platforms — Structural steel platforms designed and fabricated for onboard and offshore use.
  • Industrial Piping Systems — Stainless steel and carbon steel pipe assemblies for high-pressure marine applications.

Services Page — Welding & NDT

Welding and Non-Destructive Testing

"Certified welds. Verified integrity. No dry dock required."

Our rope access welders operate under ASME IX certified procedures, reaching hard-to-access locations where conventional methods are impractical or cost-prohibitive.

Beyond the weld, we verify it. Our NDT capabilities ensure structural integrity is confirmed before the asset returns to operation.

NDT Methods

  • LP/PT — Liquid Penetrant Testing
  • MPT — Magnetic Particle Testing
  • UT — Ultrasonic Testing
  • Thickness Measurements — Corrosion mapping and wall loss assessment
  • PAUT — Phased-Array Ultrasonic Testing (available)

All work is delivered with full documentation and traceability — compliant with classification society requirements.

💡 Why wait for major shutdowns? Rope access enables welding and inspection in hard-to-reach areas without scaffolding, without downtime, and without delays.

Services Page — Mechanical Assistance

Mechanical Assistance

"The job is at height. So are we."

In ships, offshore platforms, and refineries, mechanical maintenance rarely happens in convenient locations. Critical bolted connections, piping systems, and structural components are often positioned at height, over water, or in confined areas where conventional access is slow, expensive, or simply impossible.

Kessler Offshore delivers precision mechanical maintenance using rope access techniques — eliminating scaffolding, reducing downtime, and bringing certified technicians directly to the point of work.

Capabilities Include

  • Torque verification & bolt tensioning — performed to specification at any height
  • Rigging & load handling — controlled load movements in restricted and elevated areas
  • Piping replacement & repair — executed in live environments without scaffolding
  • Mechanical inspections — onsite assessments with full technical reporting
  • Hydraulic & electrical assistance — supporting mechanical systems onboard vessels and platforms

Our team combines expertise in rigging, mechanical, electrical/automation, and hydraulic disciplines — providing a comprehensive technical response for the maritime and offshore industry.

⚓ Even during major maintenance shutdowns, our rope access teams have made activities faster and less costly — reducing the time and cost of scaffolding erection and removal while maintaining safety and quality standards.
